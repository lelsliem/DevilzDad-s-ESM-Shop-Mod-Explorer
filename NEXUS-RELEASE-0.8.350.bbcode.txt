[size=5][b]0.8.350[/b][/size]

[b]Audio controls.[/b] Music and sound each have a volume slider and a mute checkbox in the new Audio popup. The track is independent of the visual theme, and your own .wav files work: drop one in Audio\ and pick it (anything starting with ui_ is a sound effect, not a track).

[b]Catalog.[/b] Misc and Aid now sort by the item's model as well as its name. Misc / Other is down from 1,417 items to 416, Aid / Other from 58% to 4%, and Sabacc cards from 0 to 108.

[b]Previews.[/b] Lab glass draws again, vial caps and liquid are solid and correctly coloured, and two texture-pack masks no longer print black.

[b]Before installing:[/b] run [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/starfield/mods/15632]Starfield Shader Cache Reset[/url] first to avoid a CTD from stale shader data.

[b]Updating[/b]

Replace the mod folder and keep SFShopExplorer.ini. If music was switched off before this version it stays muted - untick Mute in the Audio popup.
