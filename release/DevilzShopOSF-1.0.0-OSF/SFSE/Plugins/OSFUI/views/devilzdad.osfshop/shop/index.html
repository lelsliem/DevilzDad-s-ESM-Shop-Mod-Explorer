Track

The track is independent of the theme. Drop a .wav, .mp3, .flac or .m4a into Audio\ (or Audio\Music\) and it appears here; anything starting with ui_ is a sound effect, not a track.

Themes
Name, background and the nine colours this page draws with.

Six themes are always available. Three are earned by shopping: opens, credits spent and free hauls are counted, and progress is kept in the plugin's INI. The Custom theme unlocks once all three are earned, and the overlay's CUSTOMISE window edits the same one, including the pane layout.

Customise theme

These are the colours this page renders; everything else the theme owns stays as the overlay saved it. Art is any space_<name>.jpg (or .png, .jpeg, .bmp) in SFSE\Plugins\DevilzShopOSFAssets\ - drop one in, reopen the shop, and it is in the list.
